Local Statistics: New York City

Highlights:

  • New HIV diagnoses in 2022: 1,439, down 57% from 3,379 in 2011
  • In 2022, Black New Yorkers were diagnosed with HIV at 6 times the rate of White New Yorkers (43.2 vs 7.1 per 100,000), and Latinx/Hispanic New Yorkers at over 4 times the rate
  • Men were diagnosed at 4 times the rate of women (35.8 vs 8.8 per 100,000)
  • Viral suppression among New Yorkers with HIV reached 84% in 2022, up from 71% in 2011

Source: NYC DOHMH HIV/AIDS Annual Report, 2011 - 2022
